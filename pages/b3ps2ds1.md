# Burnout 3: Takedown - DS1 Support

Xdelta patch to remove the "DualShock 2 controller only" restriction to play the game. 

NOTE: This will disable "pressure sensitive" features permanently.

Thanks to the author(s) of [this article](https://playstationjoysticks.blogspot.com/2023/07/desbloquear-censura-ps2-dualshock1.html) for the IOP module trick. It wasn't enough though, the game executable had to be patched to recognize non-DS2 controllers properly.
